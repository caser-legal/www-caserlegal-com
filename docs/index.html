The PACER Problem

How systemic barriers in federal court access create inequity—and how CASER provides the solution.

The Challenge

The federal PACER system, designed to provide Public Access to Court Electronic Records, presents significant barriers that prevent meaningful public access to justice.

Unpredictable Search Costs

Users face unknown charges until after searches are completed, with simple typos potentially costing hundreds. This creates financial barriers to research.

Outdated Interface

The user interface remains fundamentally unusable for modern legal research, lacking intuitive design and efficient workflows.

Limited Search Capabilities

No document-level search exists. Users cannot search for legal concepts or phrases—only known case names or docket numbers.

No Accountability

Without external oversight, document availability cannot be verified, compromising transparency in the justice system.

The CASER Solution

CASER addresses these fundamental issues by providing intelligent monitoring and eliminating wasteful PACER searches through advanced technology.

Zero Search Fees

Professional Interface

Complete Transparency
